About David Tours

A local's Lisbon, not a tourist brochure

David Tours takes you through Lisbon's steep alleys, azulejo-covered façades and riverside monuments aboard a private tuk-tuk — small enough to reach corners a bus never could, and guided by someone who grew up in these streets.

Every tour is private for your group, paced to what you want to see, and filled with the stories, viewpoints and snacks that only a local knows to point out. No call centers, no rotating staff — you're booking directly with the person driving the tuk-tuk.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the €150 price per person or per tuk-tuk? +

It's a flat €150 per tuk-tuk, not per person. So solo it's €150, split between 2 it's €75 each, and a full group of 6 pays roughly €25 per person — same private tour either way.

How long does each tour last? +

Every tour — Old Town, Historical City Center and Belém — runs for approximately 2 hours.

How many people can join? +

Each tuk-tuk comfortably seats up to 6 guests, and every tour is private to your group.

How do I book a tour? +

Message us on WhatsApp with your preferred date and tour, or use the reservation form above — no account or payment required upfront.

Can I choose more than one tour in a day? +

Yes — just let us know on WhatsApp and we'll help you schedule multiple tours back to back.

Where does the tour start? +

The exact pickup point in central Lisbon is confirmed with you directly over WhatsApp once your date is set.
